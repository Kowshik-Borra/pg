Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026 | Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PG Connect collects information necessary to facilitate discoverability, bed reservation, tenancy administration, and complaint resolution. This includes:

  • Contact & Profile Data: Full legal name, verified email address, phone number, and optional college or employer details. Government identity numbers such as Aadhaar and PAN cards are not collected or stored.
  • Residency & Emergency Data: Name and contact information of designated emergency contacts and company/college affiliation for security verification.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Records of rent invoices, manual payment receipt records, security deposit status, and bank IFSC details provided by PG owners for payout validation. We do not store credit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords.
  • Location & Technical Data: Exact geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of PG properties and browser-provided location data for distance calculation when permitted by the user.
2. How Information is Used

Collected data is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • Verifying accommodation ownership and authenticity of PG properties.
  • Preventing double-booking and maintaining real-time physical bed availability.
  • Generating computer-generated monthly rent invoices and official PDF receipts.
  • Routing tenant maintenance complaints directly to authorized PG managers.
  • Maintaining immutable system audit trails to prevent fraud.
3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Designated PG Property Owners: Contact details and booking requests are shared only with the specific PG owner whose property you have requested to book.
  • OpenStreetMap & Google Maps: Property coordinates are transmitted to render mapping views and generate navigation directions.
4. Data Retention and Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and passwords are hashed using salt rounds >= 12 via bcrypt. User accounts and past invoice receipts are retained for administrative compliance and tenancy history tracking. Users can request account deactivation by contacting support.
