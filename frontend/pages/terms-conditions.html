Terms and Conditions of Service

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Platform Nature and Scope

PG Connect operates as a centralized technological platform connecting accommodation seekers (Customers/Tenants) with independent PG Property Owners and Managers. PG Connect verifies property ownership credentials and location data, but individual tenancy agreements, security deposit refunds, and daily hospitality management remain the direct responsibility of the respective PG Owner.

2. Booking Requests and Bed Reservations
  • Submitting a booking request does not automatically guarantee occupancy until reviewed and approved by the PG owner or designated manager.
  • Upon owner approval, a temporary reservation is placed on the specific physical bed for up to 48 hours. If check-in or confirmation is not recorded within this timeframe, the reservation expires and the bed is automatically returned to available inventory.
  • A customer may cancel a pending or approved booking request prior to physical check-in.
3. PG Owner Responsibilities
  • Owners must ensure all listed room details, sharing types, physical bed counts, and fee breakdowns (rent, deposit, food, electricity) represent actual conditions.
  • Owners must not artificially manipulate vacancy counts. All vacancies must correspond strictly to real physical bed states in the system.
  • Owners agree to maintain listed premises in safe, hygienic conditions and address filed maintenance complaints within reasonable timeframes.
4. Tenant Responsibilities
  • Tenants agree to adhere to property-specific house rules, notice periods (standard 30 days unless specified), and gate closing hours.
  • Tenants must provide valid emergency contact and identification credentials at the time of check-in.
  • Monthly rent must be remitted by the designated due date (typically the 5th of every calendar month).
5. Reviews and Content Authenticity

To guarantee absolute authenticity, reviews can only be submitted by verified residents with a recorded room allocation in the database. Invented or defamatory reviews are prohibited and subject to removal by platform administrators.
