About PG Connect

Solving the two fundamental friction points in paying guest accommodation: inaccurate discovery for customers, and fragmented operations for owners.

Our Objective

Every year, hundreds of thousands of students and working professionals relocate to commercial and educational hubs. Traditional discovery often suffers from inaccurate vacancy indications, opaque pricing, and misleading facilities. Simultaneously, PG owners frequently manage tenant registers, rent collections, complaints, and vacant beds through unorganized manual paper records.

PG Connect bridges this gap by unifying a transparent customer discovery portal with an enterprise-grade business management system for owners and property managers.

Physical Bed Integrity

Unlike classified listing platforms where vacancy counts are arbitrarily typed in, vacancies on PG Connect are dynamically calculated from physical bed records. When a tenant checks in, the bed is marked occupied immediately.

Zero Artificial Reviews

We reject fake testimonials and customer counters. Only tenants with an active or completed room allocation can submit reviews, guaranteeing honest community feedback.

Technology Architecture

PG Connect is built on Python 3 Flask, SQLAlchemy 2.0 with Alembic migrations, PyMySQL driver, MySQL 8.0 ACID transactions with pessimistic row-level concurrency locking, and responsive Vanilla JavaScript interfaces styled with Bootstrap 5.3.
